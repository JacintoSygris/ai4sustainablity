# IA4Sustainability

IA4Sustainability helps organise sustainability information and decisions related to the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS). It is intended for teams that need to describe an organisation, review proposed topics, record human materiality decisions and prepare a list of available and pending information.

It does not automatically generate a complete, final or guaranteed ESG or ESRS statement. Current outputs are work aids: reviewable proposals, decision sheet, CSV lists, preparation and coverage summary, summarised HTML package, guided DOCX document and, under technical conditions, an XHTML/iXBRL candidate. No output replaces assurance, legal opinion, official submission or regulatory acceptance.

## What You Need To Prepare

- Basic organisation, activity, reporting year, size and scope data.
- Internal people able to review impacts, risks, opportunities and evidence.
- Human judgement to confirm material topics and decide applicability.
- Information and evidence references when completing responses.

## Journey

1. Describe the organisation.
2. Review candidate topics proposed by the application.
3. Carry out and document double materiality with the responsible team.
4. Confirm material topics and any reasons to record.
5. Gather information, mark pending items and justify non-applicable items.
6. Review the preparation summary and download available outputs.

## Try Locally

**Warning before starting:** the included local profile recreates its SQLite database when the web component starts. Use fictitious data only. Do not use it for real information or to retain work.
