# Product

IA4Sustainability is a support application for teams managing ESG work (environmental, social and governance; ASG in Spanish) under the European CSRD/ESRS framework. Its main role is to help characterise the organisation, review proposed ESG matters for double materiality assessment, record human materiality decisions, link confirmed matters to ESRS Disclosure Requirements and indicators/data, and organise available and pending information.

It is not an automatic generator of complete ESRS reports. AI recommends and organises, but the organisation carries out and confirms double materiality, validates evidence, approves content and decides how outputs are used. The application does not certify regulatory compliance, regulatory acceptance, assurance, legal opinion or the material truth of entered data.

## Who It Is For

It is intended for people coordinating sustainability, finance, compliance, operations or advisory work who need to collect information from several areas to prepare a sustainability report. It can help create a CSRD/ESRS work structure, a pending indicators and data list, or an internal review before commissioning, completing or approving a report.

## What Information It Requests

The application may request:

- organisation identity, activity, country, currency and reporting year;
- approximate size, workforce, turnover and geographical presence;
- sector or activity through catalogues such as CNAE/NACE where available;
- review of ESG matters proposed by the system for double materiality assessment;
- human decisions on material matters and, where provided, reasons;
- structured responses to ESRS Disclosure Requirements and indicators/data;
- evidence references, such as the internal origin of a figure or a document consulted.

Entering an evidence reference does not prove that the evidence exists, is sufficient or is true. It only records the reference supplied.

## How It Is Used

The public journey should be read as tasks:

1. Describe the organisation.
2. Review the proposed ESG candidate matters.
3. Carry out double materiality analysis with your team outside or within your usual tools.
4. Confirm the final selection and the reasons you decide to record.
5. Gather ESRS indicators/data, mark pending items and justify what does not apply.
6. Review the preparation summary and download the available outputs, including the XHTML/iXBRL candidate when the conditions are met.

## What Outputs It Produces

Current outputs are named by what they contain:

- candidate matter proposals, always subject to review;
- materiality decision sheet with the saved selection;
- Disclosure Requirement, indicators/data and response list in CSV;
- preparation and coverage summary in JSON;
- printable HTML package with identification, confirmed topics, counters and limits;
- guided DOCX document with structure, guidance and spaces to complete;
- technical XHTML candidate with iXBRL tagging when the technical and validation conditions are met.

None of these outputs is, by itself, a developed ESRS report, an official submission or an accepted filing.

## What The User Still Decides

The organisation decides the scope, the interpretation of impacts, risks and opportunities, final materiality, applicability of Disclosure Requirements and indicators/data, quality of evidence, report approval and subsequent use of any download. IA4Sustainability can organise the work; it does not replace governance responsibility or professional judgement.
