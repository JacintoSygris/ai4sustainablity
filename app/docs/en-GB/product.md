# Product

IA4Sustainability is a support application for teams preparing sustainability information. Its main role is to help describe the organisation, review proposed topics, record human materiality decisions and organise available and pending information.

It is not an automatic generator of complete ESRS reports. It does not certify regulatory compliance, regulatory acceptance, assurance, legal opinion or the material truth of entered data.

## Who It Is For

It is intended for people coordinating sustainability, finance, compliance, operations or advisory work who need to collect information from several areas. It can help create a first work structure, a pending-information list or an internal review before commissioning or closing a report.

## What Information It Requests

The application may request:

- organisation identity, activity, country, currency and reporting year;
- approximate size, workforce, turnover and geographical presence;
- sector or activity through catalogues such as CNAE/NACE where available;
- review of topics proposed by the system;
- human decisions on material topics and, where provided, reasons;
- structured responses to ESRS information requests;
- evidence references, such as the internal origin of a figure or a document consulted.

Entering an evidence reference does not prove that the evidence exists, is sufficient or is true. It only records the reference supplied.

## How It Is Used

The public journey should be read as tasks:

1. Describe the organisation.
2. Review the proposed candidate topics.
3. Carry out double materiality analysis with your team outside or within your usual tools.
4. Confirm the final selection and the reasons you decide to record.
5. Gather information, mark pending items and justify what does not apply.
6. Review the preparation summary and download the available outputs.

The former internal codes P5 to P10 only appear in technical compatibility references. They are not the recommended way to navigate the product.

## What Outputs It Produces

Current outputs are named by what they contain:

- candidate topic proposals, always subject to review;
- materiality decision sheet with the saved selection;
- information list and responses in CSV;
- preparation and coverage summary in JSON;
- printable HTML package with identification, confirmed topics, counters and limits;
- guided DOCX document with structure, guidance and spaces to complete;
- technical XHTML candidate with iXBRL tagging when the technical and validation conditions are met.

None of these outputs is, by itself, a developed ESRS report, an official submission or an accepted filing.

## What The User Still Decides

The organisation decides the scope, the interpretation of impacts, risks and opportunities, final materiality, applicability of information, quality of evidence and subsequent use of any download. IA4Sustainability can organise the work; it does not replace governance responsibility or professional judgement.
