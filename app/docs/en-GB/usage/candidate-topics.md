# Review Proposed Topics

Back to the [documentation index](../index.md).

The application can propose sustainability topics from the organisation profile. These proposals are automatic and are only a starting point for human review.

## What The Proposal Contains

The proposal connects characterisation data with internal model keys and the configured topic catalogue. It may include review topics, aggregate topics or keys that are not automatically converted into a final topic.

## What It Does Not Mean

A proposal does not mean that the topic is material. The absence of a proposal does not mean that the topic is irrelevant. The decision requires review of impacts, risks, opportunities, context, evidence and professional judgement.

## How To Review It

Compare each topic with real activity, locations, value chain, affected people and financial dependencies. Add or remove topics where the analysis justifies it. Keep an explanation of important changes, especially when removing a sensitive or contextually expected topic.
