# Review Proposed Matters

Back to the [documentation index](../index.md).

The application can propose ESG matters from the organisation profile to start double materiality assessment under ESRS. These proposals are automatic and are only a starting point for human review.

## What The Proposal Contains

The proposal connects characterisation data with internal model keys and the configured ESRS matter catalogue. It may include review matters, aggregate matters or keys that are not automatically converted into a final matter.

## What It Does Not Mean

A proposal does not mean that the matter is material. The absence of a proposal does not mean that the matter is irrelevant. The decision requires review of impacts, risks, opportunities, context, evidence and professional judgement within double materiality assessment.

## How To Review It

Compare each matter with real activity, locations, value chain, affected people and financial dependencies. Add or remove matters where the analysis justifies it. Keep an explanation of important changes, especially when removing a sensitive or contextually expected matter.
