The last part of the journey shows a preparation and coverage summary. It should be read as work status, not as a developed ESRS report.

## Preparation Summary

The summary may include organisation identification, reporting year, activity code, confirmed topics, statuses, counters and limitations. It helps understand which pieces are recorded and which downloads can be attempted.

## Downloads

- **Decision sheet**: selected topics and saved reasons.
- **Information list CSV**: inventory items selected by the application.
- **Responses CSV**: structured responses and references entered.
- **Summary JSON**: preparation status, coverage and technical traceability.
- **HTML package**: printable preparation, topic and coverage summary.
- **Guided DOCX document**: structure with guidance and spaces to complete.
- **XHTML/iXBRL candidate**: technical output conditional on requirements, structured data and validation.

## How To Read Ready

A ready download means the application allows it to be generated with the current state. It does not mean the content is complete, accurate, assured, legally sufficient or accepted by a regulator.
