# Gather Indicators And Data

Back to the [documentation index](../index.md).

After material matters are confirmed through double materiality assessment, the application shows a list of Disclosure Requirements and indicators/data based on the ESRS inventory and configured mappings. That list helps organise sustainability report work, but it does not guarantee that everything required is covered in a specific case.

## Response Statuses

- **Pending**: no sufficient response has been recorded.
- **Completed**: indicators, data or explanations have been entered in the structure expected by the application.
- **Not applicable with justification**: the user has said it does not apply and supplied a justification.
- **Invalid**: a value, unit, period or required reference is missing from the format.

A completed response does not prove that the data is true. An evidence reference does not prove that the cited document is suitable. A non-applicability justification does not replace professional review.

## Coverage And Counters

Counters may add completed responses and justified non-applicable items. They must not be interpreted as a percentage of verified evidence, compliance achieved or final sustainability report preparation.

## Mappings

When a complete mapping between material matters and Disclosure Requirements is missing, the system may work in scoping mode. It then retains decisions and shows available cross-cutting blocks, but must not be presented as a complete topical list.
