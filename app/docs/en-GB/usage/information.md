# Gather Information

Back to the [documentation index](../index.md).

After topics are confirmed, the application shows an information list based on the ESRS inventory and configured mappings. That list helps organise work, but it does not guarantee that everything required is covered in a specific case.

## Response Statuses

- **Pending**: no sufficient response has been recorded.
- **Completed**: information has been entered in the structure expected by the application.
- **Not applicable with justification**: the user has said it does not apply and supplied a justification.
- **Invalid**: a value, unit, period or required reference is missing from the format.

A completed response does not prove that the data is true. An evidence reference does not prove that the cited document is suitable. A non-applicability justification does not replace professional review.

## Coverage And Counters

Counters may add completed responses and justified non-applicable items. They must not be interpreted as a percentage of verified evidence, compliance achieved or final report preparation.

## Mappings

When a complete mapping between topics and Disclosure Requirements is missing, the system may work in scoping mode. It then retains decisions and shows available cross-cutting blocks, but must not be presented as a complete topical list.
