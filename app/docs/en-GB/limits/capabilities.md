# Capability Limits

Back to the [documentation index](../index.md).

## Available

- Record an organisation profile.
- Obtain and review ESG candidate matters for double materiality assessment.
- Save human materiality decisions.
- Use a double materiality work guide.
- Inventory ESRS Disclosure Requirements, indicators/data, responses, pending items and non-applicable items.
- Download CSV, JSON and HTML summaries when the state allows it.
- Generate guided documents and certain technical outputs when their dependencies are available.

## Conditional

- The topical information list depends on configured and valid mappings.
- The XHTML/iXBRL candidate requires sufficient structured data, runtime technical validation and human approval for any later use.
- Document functions are disabled in the included public profile and the compatible extraction service is not supplied.
- Mail, OAuth, queues, persistent storage and external controls depend on the operator.

## Not Provided

- A complete, final or guaranteed ESRS statement.
- A guarantee of compliance, accuracy or regulatory acceptance.
- Assurance, audit, legal opinion or methodological approval.
- Automatic performance or autonomous confirmation of double materiality assessment.
- Independent verification of evidence.
- Data retention in the included local profile.
- Official submission to authorities.

Each operator must review the real scope of their installation before using it with business information.
