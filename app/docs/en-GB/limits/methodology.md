# Methodological Limits

Back to the [documentation index](../index.md).

IA4Sustainability organises ESG/CSRD/ESRS work, information and recorded decisions. It does not decide what is material for the organisation or guarantee that the methodology used is sufficient.

## Sources And Catalogues

The application uses catalogues, inventories and mappings versioned in the repository. Some sources are guidance or technical assets. If there is conflict with applicable standards, professional criteria or regulatory instructions, authoritative sources and expert judgement should prevail.

## Automatic Proposals

Candidate matter proposals depend on the entered profile, the included model and the configured mappings. They must be reviewed with real context within double materiality assessment. They are not universal sector statistics or decision rules.

## Double Materiality

The guide helps organise questions and evidence to determine material matters and the ESRS information that needs to be addressed. It does not itself define the number of workshops, interviews or checks required. Each organisation must justify its process according to size, activity, value chain, risks and expectations of interested parties.

## ESRS Information

The inventory helps identify Disclosure Requirements and indicators/data that may be needed. Specific applicability, level of detail, permitted omissions, final wording and any XHTML/iXBRL candidate require professional review.
