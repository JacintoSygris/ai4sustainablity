# Mappings And Catalogues

Back to the [documentation index](../index.md).

The application uses activity catalogues, topics, ESRS inventories and mappings between vocabularies. These assets help organise data, but are not by themselves regulatory authority.

## Asset Types

- CNAE/NACE activity catalogues;
- ESRS topics displayed in the interface;
- IG 3 inventory of information points;
- mappings between model keys and topics;
- mappings between topics and Disclosure Requirements;
- technical XBRL assets for conditional outputs.

## Mapping Statuses

A mapping may be approved for technical use by the application, pending review, review-only or out of scope. "Approved" in a repository file does not mean external approval or legal judgement.

## Languages

Some catalogues may contain Spanish titles in English fields where no equivalent English source is incorporated. That must not be presented as a complete translation.
